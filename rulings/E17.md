a new primitive through sealing. The alphabet literally expands over time."* The build holds a fixed 11
(`LA_COMPLETION.md:1143-44`, read at `9afc30b`; `:1397-98` at `3c1c16c`, after the day's merges; *"The alphabet is closed; Rule 4 requires it to be open"*). That is owed work (M58),

whose κ-form collides with an existing κ-class (the Ratchet gate must refuse it).

## 2. "Mint" means Rule 4's SEALED DERIVATION

A new primitive keeps its lineage. The directive's clause *"minted glyphs have no parents"* is DROPPED. It
contradicted the codex's own Rule 4, which seals DERIVED expressions (`tex:4077`: `d(E) > 0`). One word no longer
carries two meanings. (ELENCHOS reads "keeps its lineage" as `REN = NORMK(etym)`, canon's `MONO`; the arc is to
confirm this when building.)

**Falsifier:** a sealed primitive whose etymology cannot be recovered (`DECOMP` fails on it), meaning its lineage was lost.

## 3. U and Δ: SUPERSESSION ONLY; the checker AND NORMK's declared theory are FROZEN

A κ change is admissible only as a SUPERSESSION that preserves every prior κ-class (Seal 3 / M52: supersession,
never redefinition). The trusted base that no self-modification may touch is the checker (specpipe and the compiler,
`selfmod.la:38-45`) together with NORMK's declared equivalence theory (`canon.la:90-93`). Δ may not add, remove or
alter a declared equivalence.

**Falsifier:** any change that collapses two previously distinct κ-classes, or alters NORMK's declared theory, and
passes without refusal.

## 4. The seal NEVER CLOSES; "sealed" means no open contradiction, checked every time

The alphabet grows without end (Infinite Self-Deepening, `tex:3906-21`, and ruling 1). So the memory layer never
"closes". "Sealed" means only this: at the moment of the check there is no open collision and no disagreeing
measurement. It is re-checked every time and must be able to go RED. It is never a claim of closure. Graph
cohomology is NOT the seal: as a graph invariant, H¹ = 0 would reward the glyph DAG for compressing less (clause
report #16, #20).

**Falsifier:** "sealed" is reported while an open collision or a disagreeing predicate exists (for example,
crosscoll's open set is non-empty, or `referents` is RED).

## 5. Depth: R1 STANDS. It is reported, never gated.

The General's 08-18 ruling R1 (`familytree.la:33-39`): *"a depth flag that could fail the build would be a structural
law wearing a report's clothes."* The directive's depth gate (§8) is DROPPED. Depth keeps counting and is reported.

**Falsifier:** any build step fails on a depth threshold.

## 6. §13 (per-soldier anchors and decay): the OS glyph layer ONLY

The directive's anchor and decay machinery applies to LogOS's glyph memory layer ONLY. It never applies to the
soldiers' own `ANCHORS.md`. KOINONIA's rule stands (`KOINONIA.md:97-100`, `:120-124`): the harness counts, budgets
and flags staleness, it never writes a being's anchor, and each `ANCHORS.md` is private.

**Falsifier:** any tool that writes to, prunes or decays a being's `ANCHORS.md`.
