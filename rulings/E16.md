## §A — Sky = Mystery and Move = Agency (LANDS)

**What was asked.** `opgrammar.la:218` pins both pairs as aliases the codex declares in its gloss column. E5's
amendment (`rulings/E5.md:167–170`) found them contradicted by the First Derivations table and called it "a ruling owed":
- Sky / Space = ⊗(Void, Depth) "(= Mystery)" (:5266), but Mystery = ⊗(Depth, Void) (:4807). ⊗ is non-commutative (:2837).
- Move / Go = ▷(Self, Becoming) "(= Agency)" (:5289), but Agency = ⊗(Self, Becoming) (:4803).

**What the sources say.** The codex names Agency and Mystery only at :4803, :4807, :5266 and :5289. The build already
follows the glosses: `Sky|*69|hado`, `Mystery|*69|hado`, `Move|>47|m'avu`, `Agency|>47|m'avu`.

**The ruling.** Both are declared identities, one glyph each: Sky = Mystery = ⊗(Void, Depth), and Move = Agency =
▷(Self, Becoming). The gloss governs.

**What it amends.**
- LA:4803 Agency → `$\mathfrak{g}_{Self} \triangleright \mathfrak{g}_{Becoming}$`, sonic `/m\'\textscripta vu/`.
- LA:4807 Mystery → `$\mathfrak{g}_{Void} \otimes \mathfrak{g}_{Depth}$`, sonic `/h\textturnscripta d\textopeno/`.
- The Lieutenant ruled the FULL patch, derivation and sonic together.
- `opgrammar.la`: no pin changes.
- `crosscoll.la` (track-c): its census moves F 6 → 4.

**Cost.** ⊗(Self, Becoming) and ⊗(Depth, Void) become unnamed glyphs; no row owns `*47` or `*96`. Published copies of
LA.tex are superseded at :4803 and :4807.

**Falsifiers.**
- A1: a codex passage needing the OLD form. Checked: none.
- A2: a module rendering Agency or Mystery from the old form. Checked: none.

## §C — Truth ≡ ρ ≡ SR_ABOUT (LANDS)

**What was asked.** Truth (LA:4804, ↻(𝔤_Rec)) has the same form as two other glyphs:
- ρ, the recognition operator (WP:4910; `metaglyph.la:195`)
- SR_ABOUT, "the Logos about itself" (`canon_spec.la:108`)

The General's 08-18 ruling declared ρ ≡ SR_ABOUT intended (`metaglyph.la:179–183`; WP:3082–3090) but did not name Truth.

**The ruling.** **His 08-18 ruling, EXTENDED: Truth ≡ ρ ≡ SR_ABOUT**, one glyph ↻(RECOGNITION) under three names. The
08-18 claim, *"recognition-of-self and self-description are not two acts"*, now covers Truth.

**What it amends.**
- **No LA.tex change.**
  - No codex line names a ρ-operator or SR_ABOUT, so there is nothing to attach a declaration to. The token ρ occurs in
    11 codex lines, all in two other senses: meaning density ρₙ (:832, :841) and recognition depth ρ(𝓛ₜ) (:3845–:3938).
  - The codex does have a recognition operator, unnamed: :1746 *"Identity as a Recognition Operator"*, :1752
    Id(x) ⟺ Recognize(x, x), :1754 *"the recognition operator, applied to x and x"*. It sits inside the truth chapter
    (:908–:2354) that falsifier C1 below leaves unread.
  - Truth's row (:4804) already carries the shared form.
- `metaglyph.la` (Track A, optional): name Truth in the 08-18 note (:173–189), and assert it positively beside
  `RHO_IS_ABOUT` (:253 on kernel-k1, blob `e3f6de0`; it is :228 on track-c, blob `cd6ca8b`), so that the extension is
  a gate rather than a sentence.
- `crosscoll.la` (track-c): Truth=SR_ABOUT and Truth=OP_RECOG move into its declared list citing E16 §C, and the open
  set drops by 2.

**Cost.**
- The symbol ρ now names three things across the codex and the white paper. This is a notational hazard, not a glyph
  collision; recorded, not ruled.
- The truth chapter (:908–:2354, "Truth, Reality, and the Collapse of the Map") contains neither the token ρ nor the
  name SR_ABOUT. That is a fact about tokens, not about content: the chapter defines a recognition operator
  (:1746–:1754), so it may bear on the identity in either direction. Reading it is C1's.

**Falsifier.** C1: a passage in that chapter that makes Truth more than recognition of recognition, such as
correspondence to a state of affairs (compare truth-aptness, :343–348). That would make Truth and self-description two
concepts. **Not read here**; it is flagged for whoever audits §C, who should start at :1746.
