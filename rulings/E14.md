## The answer

**`Meta-X ≡ X` holds in exactly one register — metacursion at Ω-totality — and none of the
seven exceptions is in that register.** There is no contradiction to adjudicate. E14 was a
paradox in this project's technical sense: **relations sharing one word.**

### Why even the heterological three are not counterexamples

Codex I proves `Meta-X ≡ X` **∀X at Ω-totality** (`Being & Becoming.tex:5083–5090`, MC-1…MC-8,
*"the tower has no height, ∂ = 1"*). Heterologicality is a **term-level** property: a predicate
that does not apply to itself. **A term that does not apply to itself is not a totality.** The
three never enter the register the theorem quantifies over.

And their failures are *load-bearing*, which is the tell that they are diagnostics rather than
defects: `A(A) ≢ A` because *"a marker of aspirations that was itself aspirational would mark
nothing"*; meta-polemic *"does not collapse — it self-refutes"*; meta-euphemism *"cannot hide
its own hiding."* Each failure certifies that the apparatus works.

## The three relations — and they already have three notations

The dissolution needs **no new vocabulary**, which is the cheapest possible outcome and the one
the evidence supports:

1. **`≡` (TRIBAR)** — metacursive identity at Ω-totality. Codex I's claim. **Holds.** `∂ = 1`.
2. **term-level self-application** — *may* fail, and the failure is diagnostic. Three
   heterological cases, each gated (`aatc_spec.la:362,372` gates `COGITO` to **F**).
3. **`∂`** — self-validation **depth**: a *measurement*, not an identity. `∂(COGITO) = ∞`
   (`aatc_spec.la:381`, `build.sh:2589`) says COGITO's self-validation does not terminate. It
   says **nothing** about whether `Meta-COGITO ≡ COGITO`. `build.sh:1116` already records
   `DEPTH(DEPTH)` as *"the deliberate exception… the infinite descent Ω"*.

This project already separates `≡` from `=`. It had simply never stated that
**`≡`-at-totality, term-level application, and `∂` are three relations and not one word.**
Stating it is the entire ruling.

## What the paper should gain, and it is one sentence

The census at `:8543` is correct and complete as a census. It is missing the register line:
**"Of the seven, only the heterological three are failures of self-application at all, and none
of the seven is at Ω-totality, which is the register the theorem claims."** With that sentence
the counterexamples stop reading as a threat to the theorem and start reading as what the paper
already says they are — *"what make it a test rather than a stipulation."*

## Falsifiers

1. **A totality whose meta-application returns something else.** That would refute Codex I in
   its own register. None of the seven is one — all are term-level.
2. **A demonstration that heterologicality can hold of a totality.** That would move kind 1 into
   Codex I's register and reopen E14.
3. **Any use of `∂` as an identity rather than a measurement** anywhere in the build. That would
   collapse relation 3 into relation 1 and the bifurcation fails. Searched: `∂`/DEPTH is used as
   descent and as a gated exception, never as an identity predicate.
