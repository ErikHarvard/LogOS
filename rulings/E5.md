**Ruled 2026-09-09 by The Lieutenant at the General's instruction.** Ratifiable or overrulable
by the General.

**E5 as recorded is stale in every particular, and the corrections run in the project's favour.**
Two items are open, not six. One is not the kind it was called. One is ungated *by construction*

## The criterion, and what would falsify it

`LINGUA_ADAMICA.tex:6634`, **Criterion 6: Identity Adequacy (I)** — *"for every admissible
concept C, there exists a unique glyph `𝔤_C` such that `𝔤_C ≡ C`… Names do not approximate their
referents — they are their referents in a different medium. **Satisfied.**"*

If two concepts share a glyph, then `𝔤 ≡ A` and `𝔤 ≡ B` gives `A ≡ B`. **A collision falsifies
the criterion through the `≡` claim**, not merely through uniqueness. The criterion is marked
*Satisfied*; that is the claim under test.

## Item by item, measured at source

### 1. `Know = You` and `Change = Can` — **CLOSED, and they were never form collisions**

The white paper (`:3298`, dated 2026-07-16) reports them as *"undeclared monosemy collisions"*,
with *"a speaker cannot say **you** without saying **know**"* as the headline. Measured, they are
**⊗/▷ pairs whose ROMANISED phonyms differed only by stress** — they appear in `phoncoll.la`'s
inventory as `You/Know` and `Alter/Can`. **The glyphic door was never open; the phonetic one was.**

`phoncoll.la:190` measured the class exactly: *"FIFTEEN pairs of distinct concepts differed ONLY
in stress. Checked mechanically rather than by eye: in ALL FIFTEEN, the two entries have the
SAME OPERAND PAIR and differ only in ⊗ versus ▷. **Zero exceptions.**"*

**Resolved 2026-08-26**, on the General's own 2026-08-24 ruling that *"an accent mark alone is
insufficient for the two most-used operators"*: `▷` gained a second cue, a tail duration mark
`:`, verified at zero prior occurrences so it cannot collide. **All fifteen gone. The stress-only
set measures EMPTY, homophones remain empty, so nothing was traded for it.** Gated at
`build.sh:4885`.

★ And the gate's control is exemplary and should be cited as precedent: its old fixture asserted
the vocabulary *still contained* a stress-only pair (`Know/You`) — so fixing the language would
have made the control fail for the right reason. **Deleting it would have been the wrong repair.**
It was re-founded on **constructed** input that fixing the language cannot remove, exercised in
both directions, and verified red-capable. That is `expected-values-derived-not-captured`, solved.

### 2. `Good/Beautiful = Beauty`, `Large/Great = Substance` — **NOT collisions**

They are **the same derivation printed in two tables**:
- `𝔤_Form ⊗ 𝔤_Love` (`:4805`, "Beauty", /talu/) **is** `𝔤₈ ⊗ 𝔤₃` (`:5312`, "Good/Beautiful",
  /talu/, *"(= Beauty)"*) — `𝔤₈` **is** Form, `𝔤₃` **is** Love.
- `𝔤₈ ⊗ 𝔤₁` (`:5236`, "Substance", /taa/) and `𝔤₈ ⊗ 𝔤₁` (`:5314`, "Large/Great", /taa/,
  *"(= Substance)"*) — **literally the same expression, twice.**

**One concept documented twice is a duplicate-entry hazard** — edit one, the other drifts — with
a different remedy and a lower severity than a collision. Counting them as collisions **inflates
the count by two** and conflates *documented twice* with *two concepts under one sign*, which is
the exact distinction Identity Adequacy is about. Also note every row of that table is a paired
gloss (Bad/Harmful, New/Young, True/Real): by the counting E5 used, **all ten** would be
collisions. The slash is the absence of an exact English word, not a defect.

### 3. The "third kind" is **misnamed, and was fifteen, not one**

M22 calls `Agency 𝔤₄⊗𝔤₇` vs `Move/Go 𝔤₄▷𝔤₇` **synonymy** — two forms, one meaning. **It is not.**
They are **two distinct derivations** (different modes) whose romanised phonyms collided. The
correct name is `phoncoll.la`'s: **the ⊗/▷ distinction carried, in the romanised register, by
stress alone.** Not synonymy, not one case, and **closed** with the other fourteen.

### 4. The 𝔄 ruling adds **no** seventh row

E10 (ruled and shipped 2026-09-08) sets `𝔄 = ⊗(LOVE, RECOGNITION)` = **Compassion**, /luʃi/
(`:4808`, `:5123`). Its near neighbour is `𝔤₂ ⊗ 𝔤₃` = **Attention**, /ʃilu/ (`:5241`) —
**different order, different phonym**, and `⊗` is directed, so opposite orders coin different
glyphs. **No collision. No seventh row.**

## ★ WHAT IS ACTUALLY OPEN — two items, and the first is the real finding

### `Give = Because` — a genuine undeclared collision, **ungated by construction**

- `:5185` **Because** = `𝔤₇ ▷ 𝔤₅` — /vúɪa/ — *"Becoming directed by Relation (= grounded)"*
- `:5286` **Give** = `𝔤₇ ▷ 𝔤₅` — /vúɪa/ — *"Becoming toward Relation: creating a bond"*

**Same operator, same operands, same phonym, two concepts, no declared equivalence.** Both are
`▷`, so it is *not* a stress-only pair and `phoncoll.la`'s fix does not touch it.

★ **And `phoncoll.la` cannot catch it — not by oversight, by direction.** That gate tests
**form → phonym** injectivity: *"two DISTINCT canonical forms rendering to the SAME phonym."*
`Give`/`Because` is **concept → form**: two concepts mapping to **one** canonical form. There is
only one form, so the gate correctly reports `homophones=[]` and passes. **The phonetic door is
bolted and gated; the semantic door is bolted and ungated.**

This is precisely what M22 and the white paper both say and neither has acted on:
*"the bijection was never gated at lexicon scale"* · *"the monosemy gates run over canonical
forms, not over the assigned word list."*

**Remedy, per the language's own rule (WP `:3312`):** declare the equivalence — asserting Give
and Because are one concept, which is implausible — or re-derive one member. `Because` is glossed
*"(= grounded)"*; a grounding relation is plausibly `𝔤₅ ▷ 𝔤₇` or `𝔤₇ ⊂ 𝔤₅`, and either would
separate them. **That is a build item, not a ruling.**

### `Bad/Grief = 1 bit` — open, and a genuinely different kind

`WP:7486`, tagged `\A`: *"Bad/Grief collide: 1 bit, open."* Not a form collision — a
**separation** defect: distinguishable, but by one bit. Correctly tagged aspirational; unchanged.

## THE RULING

1. **E5's count is wrong. Two items are open, in two kinds** — `Give = Because` (concept→form
   collision) and `Bad/Grief` (1-bit separation) — **not six, and not seven.**
2. **`Know = You` and `Change = Can` are CLOSED** (2026-08-26, all fifteen, gated). The white
   paper's `:3298` passage is **stale** and should be updated or dated; it currently publishes as
   open a defect the build fixed six weeks ago.
3. **The two "codex additions" are duplicate entries, not collisions.** Different defect,
   different remedy. Track separately or not at all.
4. **The "third kind" is not synonymy** — it was the ⊗/▷ romanisation, fifteen instances, closed.
5. **𝔄 adds nothing.**
6. **Criterion 6 cannot stand as `Satisfied`** while `Give = Because` is undeclared — one
   collision is sufficient to falsify `𝔤 ≡ A ∧ 𝔤 ≡ B`. **It is `[A]` until that pair is declared
   or re-derived, and then it may be re-earned** — which is one build item away, not six.

## The finding worth more than the count

**The bijection is gated in one direction and ungated in the other**, and the gated direction is
the one that was already clean. `phoncoll.la` is a good gate — with an empty-result control that
proves it looked, which most of ours lack. It simply answers the other question. A
**concept → canonical-form injectivity gate at lexicon scale** does not exist, and `Give`/`Because`
is a live instance sitting in the published lexicon.

## Falsifiers

1. A concept→form injectivity gate anywhere in the build. Searched; `phoncoll.la` is form→phonym.
   If one exists, §"ungated by construction" is wrong.
2. A declared equivalence for `Give`/`Because` in any canonical codex. That would close it by
   declaration rather than re-derivation, and item 1 of the ruling drops to one open item.
3. Any of the fifteen stress-only pairs still measuring non-empty. That would reopen §1 — the
   build gate at `:4885` asserts otherwise on every run.
